🍽️ ShelfLife

Food Pantry & Emergency Preparedness Tracker

Effective Date: May 18, 2026 | Last Updated: May 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ShelfLife collects the following information to provide our food tracking and notification services:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address (via Google OAuth or registration)
  • Pantry Data: Food items, quantities, categories, and expiration dates you add to your inventory
  • Phone Number: Only if you choose to enable SMS text message notifications
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the application to improve our service

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for:

  • Displaying your personal pantry inventory and expiration alerts
  • Sending email notifications about expiring food items (if you enable this feature)
  • Sending SMS text message alerts about expiring items (only if you explicitly opt-in)
  • Improving the ShelfLife application and user experience
⚠️ SMS Notification Disclosure: By opting into SMS notifications, you consent to receive automated text messages about expiring pantry items. Message frequency varies based on your inventory. Standard message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.

3. SMS Opt-In Process

You will only receive SMS messages if you explicitly opt-in through our web form:

  1. Log into your ShelfLife account
  2. Navigate to Notification Settings
  3. Check the "SMS Notifications" checkbox (it is NOT pre-checked)
  4. Enter your valid mobile phone number
  5. Click "Save Preferences"

You can opt-out at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. After opting out, you will receive no further SMS messages.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use trusted third-party services to operate ShelfLife:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Securely stores your data (encrypted at rest)
  • Google OAuth: Handles authentication (no password storage needed)
  • Twilio: Sends SMS notifications (only if you opt-in)
  • Nodemailer/Gmail: Sends email notifications (only if you opt-in)

All third-party providers are GDPR and CCPA compliant where applicable.

5. Data Security

We protect your data using industry-standard security practices:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (10 salt rounds)
  • JWT tokens for secure, stateless authentication
  • HTTPS encryption in production (Render.com)
  • MongoDB Atlas encryption at rest and in transit
  • Environment variables for sensitive configuration

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • View: All your data is visible in your dashboard
  • Modify: Edit or update any pantry item
  • Delete: Delete individual items or your entire account
  • Opt-out: Disable email/SMS notifications in settings or reply STOP to SMS
  • Export: Request a copy of your data (contact us)

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as you maintain an active account. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our database within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

ShelfLife is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

ShelfLife is hosted in the United States. If you access the application from outside the US, your information may be transferred to and processed in the US.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of ShelfLife after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
